## Content Guidelines

### Tone
- Professional but accessible
- No jargon without explanation
- India-specific examples and context
- Avoid hyperbole ("best", "revolutionary")

### Structure
- Strong H1 with primary keyword
- Clear sections with H2s containing secondary keywords
- Bullet points for scanability
- Real examples where possible
- CTA section at the end

### SEO Requirements
- Meta description: 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword in first 100 words
- Internal links to /platform, /security, /demo (old /features and /contact-us are 301s)
- Alt text for all images

### Publishing Cadence
- Target: 2 posts per month
- Prioritize by search volume and buyer intent
